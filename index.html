Founder story
Specxy · Refurbished eyewear · Manipal, 2022

A second life for the glasses in your drawer.

Specxy was a marketplace for refurbished prescription eyewear. We bought back old specs, restored them by hand, matched them to a new owner's prescription, and priced them at about a third of a new pair.

The problem

Glasses are built to last. We throw them away as if they weren't.

  • Prescription

    Power changes, children's especially, and a perfectly good frame becomes useless overnight.

  • Wear & style

    Frames break, go missing, or simply go out of style. In 2022 India had no system to take the old pair back.

  • Speed

    The biggest online stores took 5–6 days to deliver powered lenses. An emergency spare was hard to find.

  • Price

    A decent new pair cost ₹1,500–1,800, and every new frame means more material and energy spent making it.

How it works

One pair, many owners.

Specxy ran on a closed loop. Old glasses came in as wallet points, went out as restored pairs, and came back again when the next prescription changed.

  1. Sell us your old specsA pickup partner collects the pair and checks its condition.
  2. Get Specxy Wallet pointsBuyback is paid as points (about ₹100 a pair), so the value stays in the loop.
  3. Buy refurbished, or newSpend points on a restored pair that matches your prescription, or on a new one.
  4. SubscribePlanned: a fresh pair every 12 weeks for people who like to change styles.
  5. Return the old pairIt goes back to refurbishing, and the loop starts again.
Refurbishing

Four steps from obsolete to almost new.

BeforeAfter

Before and after: frames refurbished by the Specxy team. Drag the handle to compare.

  1. 1

    Vapour honing & polishing

    A high-grit slurry under pressure strips grime and oxidation, leaving the material clean and ready for hand polishing.

  2. 2

    Plastic mending

    Heat and epoxy filling correct cracks, chips and other frame defects.

  3. 3

    Hinge repair

    The hinge is the usual failure point. Rivet and spring repairs make it work like new.

  4. 4

    Lens reglazing

    Surface scratches and imperfections are removed from the lens.

Shop by prescription, not by guesswork.

Business model

₹400 to restore. ₹500–600 to own.

We priced every rupee of a refurbished pair before pitching it. Buyback, logistics, refurbishing and storage came to about ₹400, leaving roughly ₹100 per pair, about 20% net.

Unit economics from the 2022 Pitch Tank deck. Bars drawn to scale. Hover a segment for its value.

Revenue

Three ways to earn

  • Margin on every refurbished pair sold.
  • A subscription: a fresh pair every 12 weeks, old pair returned.
  • Later, the wider eyewear and eyecare catalogue.
Customers

Who it was for

  • Children whose prescription keeps changing.
  • Working families who won't pay ₹1,500+ for a pair.
  • Teenagers who want new styles often.
  • Buyers who care about waste.
  • Anyone who needs a powered pair fast.
Key resources

What would make it defensible

  • Inventory across every power combination.
  • The four-step refurbishing process.
  • Specxy Wallet, which keeps customers in the loop.
The 2022 landscape

Lenskart, Titan Eye Plus and Vision Express sold new frames online and in stores. None of them sold restored, prescription-matched pairs. That second-hand market was Specxy's opening.

Impact

Fewer frames in the bin. Cheaper sight. Local jobs.
